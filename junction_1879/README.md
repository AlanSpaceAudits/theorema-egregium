# Four Peaks Across the Sea: the 1879 Spain-Algeria Junction

Report: [`index.html`](https://alanspaceaudits.github.io/theorema-egregium/junction_1879/)

In 1879 Spanish and French officers joined the triangulation of Spain to the triangulation of Algeria across the Mediterranean, with four summits (Mulhacén, Tetica, M'Sabiha, Filhaoussen) 82 to 270 km apart. The report follows their published record from the raw series to the final figure, then the light-flash longitude and the star work, and tests the results against a globe and a flat disc.
