# Theorema Egregium and the Shape of the Earth

### ▶ [Launch the presentation](https://alanspaceaudits.github.io/theorema-egregium/)

What distances measured on the ground say about the surface they were measured on. A slide presentation with an interactive surface lab: Gauss's measure of curvature, the ratios between a sphere and a plane, and measured railway and survey lines from Australia and Utah put through Gauss's formula.

Aether Cosmology Research Group. Pre-release draft, October 2026. Not peer reviewed. Contents are preliminary and subject to revision; not for citation without permission of the authors.

## Using it

- Open the link above, or download `index.html` and open it in a browser. It is one self-contained file and runs offline.
- Arrow keys or the buttons at the bottom change slides. F toggles full screen.
- Click any formula card, record row or fact marked ⤢ to see the cited source pages.
- `railway_lengths.kml` opens in Google Earth: the Port Augusta to Kalgoorlie line, Cook to Deakin, the Great Salt Lake trestle and the Lucin Cutoff, with their A and B ends.
