# The Hanover Triangulation, 1821 to 1824

Report: [`index.html`](https://alanspaceaudits.github.io/theorema-egregium/hanover_1821/)

Gauss's chain of triangles through the Kingdom of Hanover, from Inselsberg and Göttingen to Hamburg and Jever, followed from his collected works: the 51 observed triangles with their angle sums, the great triangle Hohehagen-Brocken-Inselsberg, the reciprocal zenith distances and refraction, the heliotrope, and the Göttingen-Altona arc. Every German and Latin quote has an English translation. Click any quote ⤢, figure ⤢ or page number to open the source page with the cited part marked.

## Contents

- `index.html`: the report, with the triangle explorer.
- `pages/`: the cited pages of Werke IV and IX.
- `hanover_triangulation_1821.kml`: the 33 main points and the sides of the 51 triangles.
- `triangles.json`: the 51 triangles as printed, with areas and sides from the printed coordinates.
